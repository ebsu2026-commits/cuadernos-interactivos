📖 Del Libro de Texto MINERD — Unidad 2, pág. 26

My Day (by Sofía)

  • Hi! My name is Sofía, and I want to tell you about my day. I usually wake up at 6:00 AM. First, I always get up and make my bed. Then, I always brush my teeth and take a quick shower. After my shower, I usually get dressed in my school uniform.
  • For breakfast, I often eat bread with cheese and drink some milk. My mom always makes delicious breakfast! After breakfast, I always pack my backpack and go to school. I never forget my books!
  • At school, we always have lunch at 12:00 PM. In the afternoon, I usually do my homework when I get home. Then, I sometimes play with my sister or watch TV. We rarely play video games during the week.
  • In the evening, we always eat dinner together as a family. After dinner, I usually read a book before I always go to bed around 9:00 PM. I always try to get a good night's sleep!

💡 En el libro (pág. 26): completen las oraciones sobre la rutina de Sofía con la información del texto.

🙋 Rules

Frases para pedir permiso, hacer preguntas y participar con respeto en clase.

  • May I go to the bathroom?¿Puedo ir al baño?
  • May I drink water?¿Puedo tomar agua?
  • Can I ask a question?¿Puedo hacer una pregunta?
  • Excuse me, may I interrupt?Disculpe, ¿puedo interrumpir?
  • I don't understand.No entiendo.
  • Can you repeat, please?¿Puede repetir, por favor?
  • Can you speak more slowly, please?¿Puede hablar más despacio, por favor?
  • How do you say ___ in English?¿Cómo se dice ___ en inglés?
  • I need help, please.Necesito ayuda, por favor.
  • May I borrow a pencil?¿Puedo pedir prestado un lápiz?
  • I'm sorry, I'm late.Perdón, llegué tarde.
  • Can I work with a partner?¿Puedo trabajar con un compañero/a?
  • Please raise your hand.Por favor, levanta la mano.
  • Listen carefully, please.Escucha con atención, por favor.
  • Please be respectful.Por favor, sé respetuoso/a.
